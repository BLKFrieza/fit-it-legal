# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

Contact: support@fit-it.app

This Privacy Policy explains how **Fit-it / FitIt Outfit Studio** handles information when you use the iOS app.

Fit-it is an outfit mockup app that allows users to capture or upload garment photos, create local outfit boards, and generate mannequin pose outputs.

This document is provided for informational purposes only and is not legal advice.

## 1. Overview

Fit-it includes two primary modes:

- **Regular Mode:** Local and deterministic outfit board creation performed on the device. Regular Mode does not use AI generation.
- **Premium Mode:** AI-assisted mannequin generation that may upload and process garment images using Firebase and Google cloud services.

Fit-it does not intentionally sell user data.


## 2. Information We May Collect or Process

Depending on how you use the app, Fit-it may collect or process:

- Garment photos you upload or capture
- Generated outfit board images
- Premium Mode generation inputs and outputs
- Purchase-related information handled through Apple StoreKit
- Basic technical or diagnostic information needed to operate or improve the app

## 3. Camera and Photo Library Access

Fit-it may request permission to access:

- Your device camera
- Your device photo library

These permissions are used only for garment image capture and garment image import.

You can manage permissions through iOS Settings at any time.

## 4. Local Storage on Device

For version 1 of the app:

- Garment images are stored locally on your device
- Regular Mode outfit boards are stored locally on your device
- Consumable credit balances are stored locally on your device

App updates should preserve locally stored credits. However, deleting the app, reinstalling the app, or switching devices may remove unused credits.

## 5. Premium Mode Cloud Processing

Premium Mode may upload and process garment images using Firebase and Google cloud services in order to generate mannequin pose outputs.

When you use Premium Mode:

- Uploaded garment images may be temporarily processed in the cloud
- AI generation requests may be processed by Firebase and Google infrastructure
- Generated outputs may be returned to your device after processing

Do not upload images that you do not have permission to use.

## 6. StoreKit Purchases

Fit-it uses Apple StoreKit for consumable in-app purchases.

Apple handles payment processing and related billing information according to Apple’s own policies.

Fit-it does not directly receive or store full payment card information.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Fit-it may use third-party services including:

- Apple StoreKit
- Firebase
- Google cloud services

These providers may process information as necessary to support app functionality.

## Advertising and Google AdMob

Fit-it may use the Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) SDK to show limited advertisements. Advertisements are only eligible to be displayed after the successful completion of a Regular Mode outfit board. Fit-it does not show ads during onboarding, garment import or capture, bulk upload, editing, Premium Mode generation, paywall or purchase flows, account screens, legal/support screens, app launch, or failed generation states.

When advertising is active, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process certain data, such as your IP address, IP-derived approximate or coarse location, device identifiers, app performance and diagnostics data, advertising data, and information about your interactions with the app and ads.

This data processing may be used for purposes including ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics, analytics, and performance.

In this release, Fit-it does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not access the device Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for personalized advertising.

Depending on your region, you may see a consent or privacy-choice prompt provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your selections in this prompt may affect ad personalization or how ads are served to you.

You can review how Google uses information from apps that use its services by visiting the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising privacy controls:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 8. Data Sharing

Fit-it does not intentionally sell user data.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate app functionality
- Process Premium Mode requests
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect users or the service

## 9. Data Retention

Local data may remain on your device until:

- You delete the app
- You remove local app content
- Your device storage is cleared

Cloud-processed Premium Mode data may be retained temporarily as needed for processing, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

Fit-it is not intended for children under 13 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact:

support@fit-it.app

## 11. User Choices

You may:

- Decline camera or photo permissions
- Use Regular Mode without using Premium Mode
- Delete local app data by removing the app
- Contact support with privacy questions

Because credits are device-local for version 1, deleting the app, reinstalling the app, or switching devices may remove unused credits.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to help protect information handled by the app and its service providers.

However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

If changes are made, the updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
